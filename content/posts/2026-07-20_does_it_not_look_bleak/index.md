Lets start by taking some time to talk about the "AI". The tools that has been popping up (Gemini, Claude and so on) are not intelligence, it is a Large Language Model. It cannot think, it cannot expand on existing ideas, it is not sentient, it has no wants or needs, it cannot do what a "normal" intelligent creature can, for the rest of the article i will reference them as what they are: LLM's

This morning i was listening to a podcast where they were referencing some articles about how an LLM was going to cause destruction of the human race by 2027, and how an LLM would decide that humans did not need to exist. And i was taken aback about this outlook on the future of LLM's, not by the thought of LLM's was going to be the downfall of humanity, but the thought of this being something that would happen within our lifetime. The end of humanity will not be fast, it will be a slow burn until the light is snuffed out, we will not notice anything until its to late.

## What is a LLM?

I am in no way going to pretend to understand the deeper crevices of how a LLM works, but as a quick way of thinking of it, its a **TEXT** prediction model, not a general prediction model. Its like a advanced version of the small suggested words that comes up when you text on your phone.

_oversimplified_

```
Query (How do i purchase milk at the store) -->
LLM model (Looks up in a HUGE very complex database) -->
Provides answer letter by letter based on the content of the databse
```

The very complex database is based on all of the text, images, audio, video and whatever else humans has created over the years that LLM companies can steal online. This include scientific papers, all of open-source, every book that has ever been digitized (and some that has not), and whatever else can be reached online. This in turn also means that all predictions that a AI can make is based on humanity providing new novel idea's. A LLM cannot create new ideas, only remix existing ones.

## But what about AGI?

The phrase Artificial general intelligence has been thrown around since OpenAI first started to get venture capital money. At worst its a bad prediction, at best its a very good marketing strategy. We have not had AGI yet, and we have not had any indication of us being closer to AGI now than we were back in 2018. In addition to this we also do not get any new or more LLM companies that tries to pretend that AGI is going to be a thing in the near future.

### What is intelligence?

I am in no way going to pretend that i have a good understanding of what intelligence is, i have my own definition and idea, but one of the core idea's that is part of my understanding is the ability to generate novel idea's, to be creative not just in implementation of existing idea's, but generating new idea's. Spoilers for later, this is becoming harder and harder for humans.

## What is going to happen?

I am in no way going to say that my prediction is correct, but if anything in life has thought us anything, downfall of humanity is not going to be guns blazing, its going to be a smolder until it's to late, i see this as there being 2 different downfalls that will happen, and they are going to build on each other.

### Short term, climate change is going to get us

We are seeing that we are living in a more and more unstable climate, we are producing so much more "frantic hand waving" everything, and we are accelerating our production of compute power that we are for the most parts wasting. We are accelerating how fast we are building giant data-centers, we are consuming more hardware than we are producing, and all of this hardware is being used to convert the power our grids cannot properly provide, and the water that should be used for humans into aunt Helga trying to convert a recipe from imperial to metric.

We are fighting a loosing battle with the climate, and i have a real fear that we will within my lifetime see climate refugees starting to migrate from new deserts created in middle Europa, India, Mexico and other parts of the world close to equator. Not because they should not deserve to live in climates that can sustain them, but because we are destroying our world.

From a normal citizens point of view, we are doing less than nothing about this issue, we are living in a timeline where we have more extreme weather year by year, but still have a large portion of the population that either does not care, or does not see reality.

### Long term, we loose what makes us humans

Lets say that the climate issue is solved, i was wrong and its not a problem, whatever solution we can put in here that allows for our current projection to continue without wavering. We are going to be having a humanity problem.

More and more teachers are yelling about students not being interested in learning, that "chat" taking over and the initiate ability to sit down and actually thinking about a problem is not being used. This is also something that grown adults are facing, its harder and harder to deal with problems at home and at work by sitting down and thinking / testing / troubleshooting instead of just giving up straight away and asking a LLM.

I for the life of me cannot think of the last time i actually sat down and tried to implement a solution to a problem i had as a small python or bash script, of course i just ask a AI. Its faster and gives me a better result that i could provide! But i learned nothing, i did not gain new knowledge about how i can do things, i did not train my brain.

(Side tagent): Do you remember back in the day when we had these "Train your brain" Nintendo DS games? And there were loads of articles and studies about how important it is to keep your brain active and plastic? Yeah, how is that going? Are you still doing this?

#### The LLM workflow

For _MOST PEOPLE_ working with AI looks something like this:

```text
User: I have a idea!
User: Writes this in a prompt to a LLM
LLM: Starts working on a response
User: Bored with slow response time, time to check TikTok
LLM: Finished
User: At some point the feeling of something important that they should be working on start to appear in the back of the mind.
```

Not only are we offloading the actual thinking, troubleshooting and brain work to a LLM, we have so little attention span that we send off our prompt, and then start to find small dopamine hits we can suck on until the LLM provides us with a answer. (Not necessarily the correct answer but a answer) We then spend time checking the results the LLM provided might might still have a good idea of the prompt we raised with the LLM, and we then start testing if the response solves the prompt we raised. And most times it does not, so we continue prompting.

```text
1: User: Ask question
2: LLM: Provide answer
3: User: Not satisfied, ask for small change
4: LLM Provide answer
5: User: Still not satisfied, return to 3
```

Not only are we only chasing the dopamine hits of a correct answer from a LLM, you loose the feeling of having created, because you have not created, you have asked something to create, your idea's are still yours, but the implementation is not. You learned nothing new.

#### And so what? If a LLM can do it, why do we need humans?

This is true, if a LLM can do a lot of what we are doing why do humans need to be involved? Its cheaper! Its faster! A LLM does not complain! A LLM does not take a smoke break in the middle of a crunch for providing new features to customers! on the small plan. But if we start to replace large parts of the working population with LLM's instead companies will loose innovation, they will start to compete more and more with each other and have the same implementations of the products.

In the long run, not 10 - 20 years, but 100 years, when the working people from this generation dies out, who will then innovate? If the younger generations does not because all they are thought is that all questions can be answered using LLM's. Who will develop new technologies, who will produce novel ideas? How will humanity evolve?

I am not just pulling this out of my behind [a brown univerisy professor changed the exam to be a in-person exam, 27 students dropped out straight away, 22 of them had a perfect 100% score, and the class average that was on 96% dropped to 48,6%](https://www.washingtonpost.com/education/2026/07/15/even-elite-colleges-are-scrambling-root-out-ai-cheating/)

We are not creating a environment where students learn, we are creating a environment where students uses tools to go around learning in order to relax. And i get that! If i was in school today, i think i would do the same thing, school sucks...
But if this continues, how are we to innovate? How are we to get new super geniuses that can create better chips for the AI industry, how are we to get new researchers that can find cures for cancer, how are we to get new people who innovate in all fields? A LLM can help the current stable of clever people, but only remix existing ideas.

## Solution! Ohh wait, should i have one?

I have none, LLM's are touching the small point in our heads that TikTok, Youtube, Instagram and all other social media has spend YEARS perfecting touching giving us instant gratification. We do not have to think, we just have to ask for solution to existing problems. I dont think LLM's are going to take over the world, as we see in Terminator with Skynet, its going to be SO much more boring, and dystopian, we will slowly loose the jobs that exist, our health will become worse and worse, our way of living will grind to a halt. And we will end up suffocating, slowly snuffing out our way of life, until we come to a point where we cannot keep LLM's, data-centers, and other "nice-to-haves" alive.

And no it wont be LLM's alone, we are actively working towards the end of civilization as we know it on many fronts, LLM's are just one part of the wheel that is driving us off the edge.

## Humanity will not disappear

Humans are like cockroaches, we are resilient, we will end up at a point where we cannot maintain the current systems running our world, they will grind to a halt, and we will need to read, learn, and rebuild based on old technologies we no longer understand.

Like in companies, change needs to come top down and not from the bottom up.
