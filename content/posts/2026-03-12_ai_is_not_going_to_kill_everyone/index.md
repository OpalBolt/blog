Lets start by taking some time to talk about the "AI". The tools that has been popping up (Gemini, Claude and so on) are not intelligence, it is a Large Language Model. It cannot think, it cannot do what a "normal" intelligent creature can, for the rest of the article i will reference them as what they are: LLM's

This morning i was listening to a podcast where they were referencing some articles about how an LLM was going to cause destruction of the human race by 2027, and how an LLM would decide that humans did not need to exist. And i was taken aback about this outlook on the future of LLM's, not by the thought of LLM's was going to be the downfall of humanity, but the thought of this being something that would happen within our lifetime. The end of humanity will not be fast, it will be a slow burn until the light is snuffed out, we will not notice anything until its to late.

## What is a LLM?

I am in no way going to pretend to understand the deeper crevices of how a LLM works, but as a quick way of thinking of it, its a **TEXT** prediction model, not a general prediction model. Its like a advanced version of the small suggested words that comes up when you text on your phone.

_oversimplified_

```
Query (How do i purchase milk at the store) -->
LLM model (Looks up in a HUGE very complex database) -->
Provides answer letter by letter based on the content of the databse
```

The very complex database is based on all of the text, images, audio, video and whatever else humans has created over the years that LLM companies can steal online. This include scientific papers, all of open-source, every book that has ever been digitized (and some that has not), and whatever else can be reached online. This in turn also means that all predictions that a AI can make is based on humanity providing new novel idea's. A LLM cannot create new ideas, only remix existing ones.

## But what about AGI?

The phrase Artificial general intelligence has been thrown around since OpenAI first started to get venture capital money. At worst its a bad prediction, at best its a very good marketing strategy. We have not had AGI yet, and we have not had any indication of us being closer to AGI now than we were back in 2018. In addition to this we also do not get any new or more LLM companies that tries to pretend that AGI is going to be a thing in the near future.

### What is intelligence?

I am in no way going to pretend that i have a good understanding of what intelligence is, i have my own definition and idea, but one of the core idea's that is part of my understanding is the ability to generate novel idea's, to be creative not just in implementation of existing idea's, but generating new idea's. Spoilers for later, this is becoming harder and harder for humans.
